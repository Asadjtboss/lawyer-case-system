Advocate Drafting Library

Petition

Bail Application Draft

IN THE COURT OF: Hon'ble Sessions Judge, Attock

CASE TITLE: Mst. A vs State

APPLICATION FOR BAIL: Applicant/Accused respectfully submits that the applicant is innocent and has been falsely implicated.

The applicant is neither a previous convict nor a flight risk. The allegations are not supported by concrete evidence and the investigation is complete. The applicant undertakes to appear before the court as and when required and to comply with all legal conditions.

It is therefore prayed that the applicant may be admitted to bail subject to such terms and conditions as this Hon'ble Court deems fit and proper in the interests of justice.

Civil

Civil Suit Title / Writ Draft

IN THE COURT OF: Civil Judge, Attock

PLAINTIFF: [Name], son of [Parent], resident of [Address]

DEFENDANT: [Name], resident of [Address]

The plaintiff states that the subject property is in his lawful possession and ownership. The defendant has interfered with lawful possession and created a threat of dispossession without any legal basis. The claim is supported by documentary evidence, site inspection, and witness testimony.

It is therefore prayed that the suit be decreed in favour of the plaintiff, the defendant be restrained from interference, and all consequential relief be granted.

1. The contents mentioned in the accompanying application are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief.

2. I am the lawful owner/authorised person in respect of the subject matter and I am competent to swear this affidavit.

3. I have not concealed any material fact and no part of the statement is false or misleading.

DEPONENT

VERIFICATION: Verified at [City] on [Date] that the contents are true and correct.

Notice

Legal Notice Draft

LEGAL NOTICE: Through this notice, [Receiver Name] is called upon to perform/cease the act described below within [X] days from the receipt of this notice.

It is stated that the undersigned has suffered loss and injury due to the acts/omissions of the addressee and the same are unlawful, unjustified, and contrary to law.

Failure to comply shall leave the undersigned with no option but to initiate appropriate legal proceedings including but not limited to filing a civil/criminal complaint before the competent forum.

Yours faithfully, [Advocate Name], Advocate.

Appeal

Review Petition Draft

IN THE HONOURABLE HIGH COURT OF [Province]: Review Petition No. ___ of ___

Petitioner: [Name], son of [Parent], resident of [Address]

Respondent: [Name], resident of [Address]

The petitioner respectfully submits that the Hon'ble Court, while deciding the matter, has committed an error apparent on the face of the record which requires correction in the interest of justice.

The impugned order suffers from material factual and legal misapprehension and the petitioner has no efficacious alternative remedy except this review petition. It is therefore prayed that the order be reviewed and the relief sought be granted.
